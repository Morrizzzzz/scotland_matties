THE CARTOGRAPHER’S NOTES

Real journeys.
A little enchantment.

A fantasy-inspired atlas of your Scotland recordings. The landscape is real; the atmosphere is imagined.

What is on the map?

All 24 records from EHW, SSW and WHW in SCOTLAND_HIKING.zip: 53,129 vertices with the original attributes, heights and multipart boundaries. Route names are inferred from the layer codes and locations.

These recordings date from 2014–2016 and include detours and side trips. Distances are approximate horizontal distances along the supplied geometry, not official trail lengths. Gaps are not joined.

Elevation & terrain

The profile uses the shapefiles’ original Z values, assumed to be metres. Their vertical datum and accuracy are not specified. The 3D landscape uses a separate public elevation model and an adjustable height exaggeration.

Map & credits

Built with MapLibre GL JS, with a Leaflet 2D fallback for browsers without WebGL. Geographic backdrop: Natural Earth (public domain). Terrain: Mapzen Terrain Tiles on AWS, with source credits. Fonts: Cinzel and Manrope (SIL Open Font License).

The backdrop is deliberately a small-scale atlas. Live terrain requires an internet connection. Historical recordings and this stylised map are for exploration, not current trail navigation.
